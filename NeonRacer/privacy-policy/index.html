Privacy Policy

NEONRACER

Infinite Lane Dodge Arcade Game

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Privacy at a glance: NeonRacer is an offline game that does not require an account and does not ask users to provide personal information. The game does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information with the developer or third parties.

1. About NeonRacer

NeonRacer is a simple offline arcade game. You can play the game without creating an account, signing in, or providing personal information.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information.

NeonRacer does not require or collect information such as:

  • Name or username
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Postal address
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Contacts
  • Photos, camera, or microphone data
  • Account passwords or login information
  • Advertising identifiers

3. Device and Usage Data

NeonRacer does not use analytics, tracking technologies, advertising networks, or third-party data collection services to collect information about how you use the game.

4. Information Stored on Your Device

The game may store gameplay data such as your best score locally on your device so that it can be displayed again when you return to the game. This local gameplay data is not submitted to the developer and is not used to identify you.

5. Internet Connection and Third-Party Services

NeonRacer is designed to work offline. It does not require an internet connection to play and does not send gameplay or personal information to the developer.

NeonRacer does not include third-party advertising, analytics, social-login services, or other external services that collect personal information.

6. Permissions

NeonRacer does not require access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, files, or other personal information in order to play the game.

7. Children's Privacy

Because NeonRacer does not require users to create accounts or provide personal information, the game is designed to minimize privacy risks for players of all ages. Parents or guardians who have questions about the game's privacy practices may contact the developer.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game or its privacy practices change. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
